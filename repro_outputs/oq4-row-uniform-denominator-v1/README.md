# OQ4 varying-row uniform energy and random denominator asymptotics

This milestone adds two complete modules for varying row laws with a common bound on the absolute third moment. RowUniformConditionalEnergy proves simultaneous relative control over the actual deterministic strip energy on the full radius interval, a boundary probability bound, and concentration at the original energy scale. RowRandomDenominatorAsymptotic uses those estimates to prove the signed center limit, vanishing whole middle and far contributions, and the final actual random-denominator event/strip-probability ratio limit.

The row law may vary without convergence to a fixed law. The scale hypotheses, actual finite-product measures, radius ranges and source moment assumptions are preserved in the frozen declarations. The middle estimate controls the growing finite family uniformly; the far estimate pays the denominator and dyadic sum using the actual common third-moment bound. The final squeeze fixes epsilon before passing epsilon to zero.

Both exact sources have a first successful server kernel check, an audit of every actual owned declaration with recursive TYPE and VALUE axiom closure, and a qualified independent complete source-to-statement/proof review. The original objects are retained on the server and reused without rechecking old producers. The C module has 12 actual owned declarations (3 public, 2 explicit private, 7 generated); D has 31 (4 public, 6 explicit private, 21 generated). All 43 are theorems; their full TYPE export totals 1782 LF. No non-theorem value is introduced. Recursive axioms are limited to propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

The prior positive-boundary and strip-energy producers are already accepted at v123/PR457. This milestone does not change their source or any previously accepted file. The two new modules import those exact producers and the qualified original finite estimates. The original fixed-law OQ4 limited scope remains complete at v79. The broader varying-row Brownian closed-window theorem remains open pending the separate RowWindowProduct module; it is not claimed by this milestone.

Root intake may reuse both full independent reviews and both first original objects by exact source/object/interface bindings.
