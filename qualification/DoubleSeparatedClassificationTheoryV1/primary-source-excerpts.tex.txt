4778: \begin{lemma}[A gradient ratio from the terminal trace]\label{d:ratio}
4779: If $x\ge16$, $a\ge x\sqrt T$, $y=a-u$, $0<u\le|b|\le\sqrt{T/2}$,
4780: $|z|<a$ and $|z-y|\le3x\sqrt T/2$, then
4781: \begin{equation}\label{d:ratiobound}
4782:  0\le f_y^I-f_y^+\le Ce^{-a^2/T}(-f_y^+).
4783: \end{equation}
4784: \end{lemma}
4785: \begin{proof}
4786: Scale $A=a/\sqrt T$, $U=u/\sqrt T$, $V=(a-z)/\sqrt T$.
4787: Then $U\le1/\sqrt2$ and $U+V\le\sqrt2+3x/2\le13A/8$.
4788: For $q=f^I-f^+$ in these scaled variables, both kernels have zero
4789: terminal trace at $V=0$, and their normally convergent image series
4790: give $q_U(U,0)=0$. After subtracting the upper half-line, its exponents are
4791: \[
4792:  E_m=8m^2A^2+4mA(U-V),\quad
4793:  F_j=8j^2A^2-4jA(U+V)+2UV,\qquad m,j\ne0.
4794: \]
4795: For $0\le V'\le V$ they are at least $3k^2A^2/2$, where
4796: $k=|m|$ or $|j|$; their first derivatives are $O(kA)$ and mixed
4797: derivatives $O(1)$. Hence, uniformly on this whole segment,
4798: \[
4799:  |q_{UV}(U,V')|\le C\sum_{k\ge1}(1+k^2A^2)e^{-3k^2A^2/2}
4800:  \le Ce^{-11A^2/8}.
4801: \]
4802: Normal convergence also justifies integration from the zero trace,
4803: giving $|q_U(U,V)|\le CVe^{-11A^2/8}$. The sign is
4804: Lemma~4.2. Divide only now by
4805: $-\sqrt T f_y^+=2Ve^{-2UV}>0$.
4806: Since $2UV\le13A/(4\sqrt2)\le3A^2/16$ for $A\ge16$,
4807: the ratio is at most $Ce^{-19A^2/16}\le Ce^{-A^2}$.
4808: \end{proof}

5433: \subsection{Every separated double-crossing overshoot}
5434: All quantities in this subsection retain the original strict live
5435: prefix, interior terminal endpoint, small-energy crossing, and
5436: $r<\kappa_0$ indicators before a pointwise upper bound is taken.
5437: At an upper crossing put $j=r+1$, $e=S_j-a$, $b=X_j$, and let
5438: $U,d'$ be the actual square sum and sum strictly after $j$. Write
5439: \[
5440:  W_{n-j}=1_{\{U\ge2\beta^2\}}U^{-1/2}(1+|d'|/\sqrt U)^2.
5441: \]
5442: It is zero on its excluded branch. With $u=a-S_{j-1}=b-e$,
5443: $T=b^2+U$ and $v=a-S_n=-e-d'>0$, the exact nonnegative overshoot is
5444: \begin{equation}\label{d:exactO}
5445:  O_{j-1}^+=ec\frac{2v}{T}e^{-2uv/T},\qquad
5446:  c=1-(b+u)(b+d')/T\ge0.
5447: \end{equation}
5448: Small energy implies $b^2\le U$, $U\ge h_0/2$; terminal interiority
5449: gives $v\le|d'|$, and $c\le3(1+|d'|/\sqrt U)$.
5450: The actual first upper crossing has $0\le e\le D_j$. Hence
5451: \begin{equation}\label{d:Oenvelope}
5452:  O_{j-1}^+\le6D_jW_{n-j},
5453:  \qquad\text{a previous lower hit implies }L_j\ge S_j+2a\ge3a.
5454: \end{equation}
5455: The latter witness is the prefix arc ending at that lower hit; all
5456: objects in its eventual cyclic averaging are rotation invariant.

5496: \begin{lemma}[Second-wall and bad-displacement one-wall budgets]
5497: \label{d:allO}
5498: Under \eqref{d:resregime},
5499: \begin{align}\label{d:Ooutputs}
5500:  E\sum_{r<\kappa_0}O_r^+1_{\{\min_{k\le r}S_k\le-a\}}
5501:  &\le C\delta\Psi(x),\nonumber\\
5502:  E\sum_{r<\kappa_0}O_r^+1_{\{|S_n-S_r|\ge3x\sqrt{T_r}/2\}}
5503:  &\le C\delta\Psi(x).
5504: \end{align}
5505: Reflection gives both lower-wall budgets. Moreover
5506: $E\mathcal O_D(\kappa_0)\le C\delta\Psi(x)$.
5507: \end{lemma}
5508: \begin{proof}
5509: We first pay every upper one-wall overshoot for $j\le\gamma_0n$,
5510: where $\gamma_0>0$ is a sufficiently small fixed fraction.
5511: On $Q\ge n/2$, its actual strip is $0\le F_j\le D_j$ and
5512: $S_j\ge A=x\sqrt{n/2}$. Every positive-energy crossing has
5513: $j\ge x^2$ by $S_j^2\le jQ_j\le jQ$.
5514: Taking $X_0\ge4$, $\gamma_0\le1/2$ ensures
5515: $j\ge12$, $A\ge3\sqrt j$ and $A\ge24B$.
5516: Condition on the complete true suffix. Equations~\eqref{d:Oenvelope},
5517: \eqref{d:endstripbound} and \eqref{d:Wsum} give
5518: \[
5519:  E[O_{j-1}^+;Q\ge n/2]
5520:  \le C\frac\beta{j\sqrt n}
5521:         e^{-A(100B)^{-1}\log(1+AB/j)}.
5522: \]
5523: Put $\gamma=j/n$, $d=\eta/\sqrt2$ and
5524: $q=x^2/(200\sqrt2\eta)$. Choose
5525: $\gamma_0\le\min(1/2,d^2,e^{-400\sqrt2\eta})$ and
5526: $X_0^2\ge200\sqrt2\eta$.
5527: Then $\log(1+d/\gamma)\ge\frac12\log(1/\gamma)$,
5528: the exponential is at most $\gamma^q$, $q\ge1$, and
5529: $\gamma_0^q\le e^{-2x^2}$.
5530: For $J=\lfloor\gamma_0n\rfloor$,
5531: $n^{-q}\sum_{j\le J}j^{q-1}\le(J/n)^q\le\gamma_0^q$.
5532: Thus all these early overshoots cost $C\delta e^{-2x^2}$.
5533: Lemma~\ref{d:W} pays every low-$Q$ branch without an activity mask.
5534: 
5535: For $j\ge\gamma_0n$, put $\theta_0=2x/\sqrt n$ and split into
5536: $k=\lfloor j/2\rfloor,l=j-k$. Choose $\eta\le1/200$ and then
5537: $X_0$ so $\theta_0\sqrt k,\theta_0\sqrt l\ge1$.
5538: Apply Lemma~\ref{d:weightedtools} to $[-S_B,D_B-S_B]$:
5539: the actual-statistic slopes were checked in Lemma~\ref{d:endstrip},
5540: and its bad-count proof is uniform in $k$. Thus, uniformly in the true suffix,
5541: \begin{equation}\label{d:smallballot}
5542:  E[D_je^{\theta_0L_j};0\le F_j\le D_j]
5543:  \le C_{\gamma_0}\frac\beta n(1+x)^5
5544:  e^{.506j\theta_0^2+Cj\theta_0^3\beta+C\theta_0B}.
5545: \end{equation}
5546: The previous lower hit supplies $L_j\ge3a$. On $Q\ge n/2$ its
5547: exponent gap is $3\sqrt2-4(.506)>2.2$; cubic and support costs are
5548: bounded numerical factors. Multiplying by $W_{n-j}$ and summing
5549: \eqref{d:Wsum} proves the first line of \eqref{d:Ooutputs}, since
5550: any fixed polynomial times $e^{-2.2x^2}$ fits $C\Psi(x)$.
5551: 
5552: For the second line, first restrict to $r<\tau$. Its $j=r+1$ has
5553: $t=n-j\ge m$ and $U\ge H/2$. Deleting the crossing label in
5554: $|b+d'|\ge3x\sqrt{b^2+U}/2$ forces
5555: $|d'|\ge\sqrt{9x^2/4-1}\sqrt U$ by the deterministic Cauchy
5556: inequality in Lemma~\ref{d:rare}. The ordinary record-strip bound \eqref{d:GWB}
5557: is $C\beta/j$, uniform in that actual future. Equation~\eqref{d:FUT}
5558: therefore costs $C\beta\Psi(x)/(j\sqrt t)$ at each remaining rank.
5559: For $j\ge\gamma_0n$, summing $t^{-1/2}$ pays $C_{\gamma_0}\delta\Psi(x)$.
5560: The early ranks and low full energy were already paid without this
5561: mask. Restore $\tau\le r<\kappa_0$ using the nonnegative omitted
5562: one-wall budget in Lemma~\ref{d:cutoff}.
5563: 
5564: It remains to check every double-hit overshoot in
5565: \eqref{d:doubletel}. At a first interval exit with regular displacement
5566: and interior terminal endpoint, \eqref{d:ratiobound} multiplies its
5567: actual nonnegative one-wall overshoot pointwise. Lemma~\ref{d:OW}
5568: then pays $C\delta e^{-x^2}$. At a bad first exit the exact magnitude
5569: is $O_r^+-O_r^I\le O_r^++(O_r^I)_-$; the second term must be kept.
5570: The first is the second line of \eqref{d:Ooutputs}. The projection
5571: bound \eqref{cn:projectbound} pays the negative interval term cubically.
5572: Early ranks use the first rare grid with drift power three. At late
5573: ranks its crossing support has wall gap at most $|b|$; condition on
5574: the complete real suffix and apply \eqref{d:wallstrip}, giving
5575: \[
5576:  C1_{\{T\ge h_0,\ |d|\ge3x\sqrt T/2\}}
5577:  |b|^3T^{-3/2}D^3(T/r+\beta^2/r).
5578: \]
5579: The parenthesis is at most $5T/(2n)$, and the second rare grid pays it.
5580: An opposite exterior terminal endpoint makes a regular first crossing
5581: impossible, since $|z-y|\ge(2-1/x)a>3x\sqrt T/2$; the crossed-side
5582: exterior endpoint makes the active completions zero.
5583: After one prior hit only the negative remaining one-wall overshoot
5584: occurs, paid by the first line of \eqref{d:Ooutputs}; after both hits
5585: none occurs. Equality has $e=0$, and large-energy crossings remain
5586: retained. These cases prove the final assertion without the invalid
5587: full-amplitude interval/one-wall domination.
5588: \end{proof}
