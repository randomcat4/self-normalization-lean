
\begin{lemma}[All-level transfer and absolute comparison]\label{d:OW}
In the bounded regime of Lemma~\ref{d:strip}, if
$\delta(1+x)^3\le\epsilon$ for a sufficiently small fixed
$\epsilon$, then uniformly in $x\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{d:OWbound}
 E\sum_{r<\kappa_0}O_r^\pm\le C\delta.
\end{equation}
The retained and negative-interval terms have absolute total at most
$C\delta$, and the actual-$\kappa_0$ terminal error is $C\delta^2$.
Moreover
\begin{equation}\label{d:allabsolute}
 |P(M_n^a\ge x\sqrt Q)-H(x)|+|P(D_n(x))-d(x)|\le C\delta.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We verify the three budgets of Lemma~4.3 at the common
$H=h_0$, $\nu=\kappa_0$. Lemma~\ref{cn:localprofile} holds at
every $a>0$. Early retained and interval-negative terms use
$\Pi\le2$ and the first grid in \eqref{d:grids}. At each late
deterministic rank the layer-cake calculation of
Proposition~\ref{cn:paid}, with \eqref{d:wallstrip}, bounds them by
\[
 C1_{\{T\ge h_0\}}|b|^3T^{-3/2}D^5(T/r+\beta^2/r).
\]
On $r\ge n/2,T\ge h_0=4\beta^2$ the parenthesis is at most
$5T/(2n)$. Marked-coordinate averaging and the second grid in
\eqref{d:grids}, with $m=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$, give $B=C\delta$;
all other factors are symmetric in the genuine suffix labels.

Here $R_n(0)\le\delta$, so $h\le h_0$. The strip
\eqref{d:wallstrip} supplies \eqref{cn:terminalstrip} for $H=h_0$;
Lemma~\ref{cn:terminal} at $m=0$ gives $E=C\delta^2$.
The initial half-line test has variation one. Scalar
\eqref{cn:scalar} and \eqref{cn:zero} compare its expectation to
$1-2\Psi(x)$ within $C\delta$. The support implies
$R_n(x)=\delta(1+x)^3$ (take $\eta\le1/2$), so C1 is legal at
this actual budget, and its error is at most
$C\delta(1+x)^3\Psi(x)\le C\delta$. Thus $G=C\delta$.
Lemma~4.3 proves \eqref{d:OWbound} and the absolute
interval comparison to its initial completion. Lemma~\ref{d:initial}
compares that completion to Brownian survival within $C\delta$.
Exact weak-event inclusion--exclusion and the same legal C1 for both
signs give the double-hit comparison in \eqref{d:allabsolute}.
Strict survival complements weak crossing, also on $Q=0$.
\end{proof}
\subsection{Rare marked cubic and unmarked-future budgets}
\begin{lemma}[Rare real-suffix budgets]\label{d:rare}
In \eqref{d:resregime}, choose $K$ once for the finitely many fixed
drift and inverse powers used below. For each such $p\ge0$,
\begin{align}\label{d:raregrids}
 \sum_{t\ge n/2}E[1_{\{T_t\ge h_0\}}|X_1|^3T_t^{-3/2}D_t^p;
                         |d_t|\ge3x\sqrt{T_t}/2]
 &\le C_p\delta\{\Psi(3x/2)^{1/2}+e^{-x^2}\},\nonumber\\
 \frac1n\sum_{t\le n/2}E[1_{\{T_t\ge h_0\}}|X_1|^3T_t^{-1/2}D_t^p;
                         |d_t|\ge3x\sqrt{T_t}/2]
 &\le C_p\delta\Psi(x).
\end{align}
For $t\ge m$, $U=\sum_{i=1}^tX_i^2$, $d'=\sum_{i=1}^tX_i$,
$v_x=\sqrt{9x^2/4-1}$, one also has
\begin{equation}\label{d:FUT}
 E[1_{\{U\ge H/2\}}U^{-1/2}(1+|d'|/\sqrt U)^2;
                          |d'|\ge v_x\sqrt U]
 \le Ct^{-1/2}\Psi(x).
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
It remains to check every double-hit overshoot in
\eqref{d:doubletel}. At a first interval exit with regular displacement
and interior terminal endpoint, \eqref{d:ratiobound} multiplies its
actual nonnegative one-wall overshoot pointwise. Lemma~\ref{d:OW}
then pays $C\delta e^{-x^2}$. At a bad first exit the exact magnitude
is $O_r^+-O_r^I\le O_r^++(O_r^I)_-$; the second term must be kept.
The first is the second line of \eqref{d:Ooutputs}. The projection
bound \eqref{cn:projectbound} pays the negative interval term cubically.
Early ranks use the first rare grid with drift power three. At late
ranks its crossing support has wall gap at most $|b|$; condition on
the complete real suffix and apply \eqref{d:wallstrip}, giving
\[
 C1_{\{T\ge h_0,\ |d|\ge3x\sqrt T/2\}}
 |b|^3T^{-3/2}D^3(T/r+\beta^2/r).
\]
The parenthesis is at most $5T/(2n)$, and the second rare grid pays it.
An opposite exterior terminal endpoint makes a regular first crossing
impossible, since $|z-y|\ge(2-1/x)a>3x\sqrt T/2$; the crossed-side
exterior endpoint makes the active completions zero.
After one prior hit only the negative remaining one-wall overshoot
occurs, paid by the first line of \eqref{d:Ooutputs}; after both hits
none occurs. Equality has $e=0$, and large-energy crossings remain
retained. These cases prove the final assertion without the invalid
full-amplitude interval/one-wall domination.
\end{proof}
