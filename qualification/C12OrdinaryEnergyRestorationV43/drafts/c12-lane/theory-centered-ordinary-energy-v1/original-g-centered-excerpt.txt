1403: \begin{lemma}[Centered comparison with ordinary energy]\label{g:centered}
1404: There are numerical $\eta_G,\epsilon_G,C>0$ such that if
1405: $EZ=0$, $EZ^2=1$, $\beta=E|Z|^3$, $e_n=\beta/\sqrt n$, and
1406: \[
1407:  y\ge1/2,\qquad |Z|\le\eta_G\sqrt n/y,\qquad e_ny^3\le\epsilon_G,
1408: \]
1409: then
1410: \begin{equation}\label{g:centered-result}
1411:  |P(D_n^Z(y))-d(y)|\le Ce_n(1+y)^3\Psi(y).
1412: \end{equation}
1413: \end{lemma}
1414: \begin{proof}
1415: For $1/2\le y\le1$, $R_n(0)\le e_n$ and
1416: $R_n(y)\le(1+y)^3e_n\le64\epsilon_G\le\rho_*$.
1417: Apply \eqref{cn:CP} and legal C1, \textup{(S.8.1)}, to both signs.
1418: Exact weak-event inclusion--exclusion gives error $Ce_n$;
1419: $(1+y)^3\Psi(y)$ has a positive numerical lower bound on this interval.
1420: For $y\ge1$ write $u=e_n(1+y)^3\le8\epsilon_G$.
1421: For $1\le y\le X_0$, apply Lemma~\ref{d:fixed}.
1422: For $y>X_0$ and $u\ge c_*e^{-3y^2}$, use
1423: Lemma~\ref{d:nonres}; in the complementary residual range use
1424: Proposition~\ref{d:residual}.
1425: \end{proof}
