1429: \begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{g:main}]
1430: Put $\xi=X/(\sigma\sqrt n)$ and $F=f$, with $f$ as in
1431: \eqref{m:R}; thus $R_n(x)=nEF((1+x)\xi)$.
1432: First choose $\epsilon_c\le\min(1/4,\eta_G/(2\sqrt2))$, then
1433: $\eta_c>0$ small enough below, all numerical. The case
1434: $R_n(x)\ge\eta_c$ follows at once from \eqref{g:host-coarse} and
1435: $d(x)\le2\Psi(x)$, so assume $R_n(x)<\eta_c$.
1436: For $a=1+x$, let
1437: \[
1438:  h_x=\epsilon_c/a,\quad \mathcal N_x=\bigcap_{i\le n}\{|\xi_i|\le h_x\},
1439:  \quad p_x=P(\mathcal N_x),\quad
1440:  A\mathrel{\overset d=}\xi\mid\{|\xi|\le h_x\}.
1441: \]
1442: Let $\pi=P(|\xi|>h_x)$ and $v_{\rm tail}=nE[\xi^2;|\xi|>h_x]$.
1443: For $|z|>\epsilon_c$,
1444: $F(z)\ge\epsilon_c^3$, $F(z)\ge\epsilon_c z^2$ and
1445: $F(z)\ge\epsilon_c^2|z|$, so
1446: \begin{equation}\label{g:conditional-bills}
1447:  n\pi\le\epsilon_c^{-3}R_n(x),\quad
1448:  v_{\rm tail}\le\epsilon_c^{-1}a^{-2}R_n(x),\quad
1449:  nE[|\xi|;|\xi|>h_x]\le\epsilon_c^{-2}a^{-1}R_n(x).
1450: \end{equation}
1451: If $R_n(x)<\eta_c\le\epsilon_c^3/4$, then $p_x\ge1-n\pi\ge3/4$
1452: and the exact conditional product law has iid increments $A$, with
1453: \begin{equation}\label{g:conditional-moments}
1454:  nEA^2\in[3/4,4/3],\quad
1455:  n|EA|\le2\epsilon_c^{-2}a^{-1}R_n(x),\quad
1456:  nE|A|^3\le2a^{-3}R_n(x).
1457: \end{equation}
1458: Indeed $nEA^2=(1-v_{\rm tail})/(1-\pi)$ and
1459: $EA=-E[\xi;|\xi|>h_x]/(1-\pi)$; on the retained set
1460: $F(a\xi)=a^3|\xi|^3$. The generally nonzero mean is retained.
1461: 
1462: Put $\mu=EA$, $C_i=A_i-\mu$, $v=nEA^2$,
1463: $d_0=n|EA|$, $g=nE|A|^3$ and
1464: $\Lambda=ad_0+a^3g\le C_{\epsilon_c}R_n(x)$.
1465: Choose $\eta_c$ so this is sufficiently small for the following
1466: numerical requirements. For $v_0=nEC^2=v-d_0^2/n$ and
1467: $B=C/\sqrt{v_0}$,
1468: \begin{equation}\label{g:recenter-moments}
1469:  v_0\in[1/2,4/3],\quad nEB^2=1,\quad
1470:  \delta=nE|B|^3\le C_0g,\quad |C|\le2\epsilon_c/a.
1471: \end{equation}
1472: Indeed $nE|C|^3\le4n(E|A|^3+|\mu|^3)\le8g$.
1473: The support and $v\ge3/4$ give
1474: $x/\sqrt n\le2\epsilon_c/\sqrt3\le1/2$. Thus
1475: \[
1476:  h=3d_0\ge2d_0(1+x/\sqrt n),\qquad
1477:  ah\le3\Lambda,\qquad x-h\ge1/2,\quad x+h\le a.
1478: \]
1479: 
1480: For $0<s<t$, the Brownian double-hit difference lies in
1481: $\{s\le\max B<t\}\cup\{s\le-\min B<t\}$; reflection gives
1482: $0\le d(s)-d(t)\le4\{\Psi(s)-\Psi(t)\}$.
1483: Since $ah$ is small, the Mills bounds give $\Psi(x\pm h)\asymp\Psi(x)$ and
1484: \begin{equation}\label{g:threshold-payment}
1485:  |d(x\pm h)-d(x)|\le Cah\Psi(x)\le C\Lambda\Psi(x).
1486: \end{equation}
1487: For $E=\{Q_C\ge v_0/2\}$ use the Laplace calculation in the proof
1488: of Lemma~\ref{d:nonres} for the actual $B$, with $nEB^2=1$,
1489: $\delta=nE|B|^3>0$, deficit $\kappa=1/2$ and $s=(4\delta)^{-2}$.
1490: Its exponential split and lower Mills payment, at this law's own
1491: $u'=\delta a^3\le C_0\Lambda$, yield
1492: \begin{equation}\label{g:bad-center-energy}
1493:  P(E^c)\le e^{-1/(64\delta^2)}\le Cu'\Psi(x)
1494:        \le C\Lambda\Psi(x).
1495: \end{equation}
1496: 
1497: Pathwise, $|S_k^A-S_k^C|\le d_0$ for every $k$, and
1498: $|\sqrt{Q_A}-\sqrt{Q_C}|\le d_0/\sqrt n$ by the norm inequality.
1499: On $E$, $\sqrt{Q_C}\ge1/2$ and
1500: $h\sqrt{Q_C}\ge d_0(1+x/\sqrt n)$. Both signed weak inequalities
1501: therefore give
1502: \[
1503:  D_n^C(x+h)\cap E\ \subseteq\ D_n^A(x)\cap E
1504:  \ \subseteq\ D_n^C(x-h)\cap E,
1505: \]
1506: and hence
1507: \[
1508:  P(D_n^C(x+h))-P(E^c)\le P(D_n^A(x))
1509:  \le P(D_n^C(x-h))+P(E^c).
1510: \]
1511: The middle event retains the actual $Q_A$. For $Z=\sqrt nB$, its own
1512: centered budget is $e_n=\delta$, and at each $z=x\pm h$,
1513: \[
1514:  |Z|\le2\sqrt2\epsilon_c\sqrt n/a\le\eta_G\sqrt n/z,
1515:  \qquad \delta z^3\le C_0ga^3\le C_0\Lambda\le\epsilon_G.
1516: \]
1517: Choose $\epsilon_c$ after $\eta_G$ and $\eta_c$ after both
1518: $\epsilon_c,\epsilon_G$ to ensure these conditions and the preceding
1519: smallness requirements. Lemma~\ref{g:centered} applies at these two
1520: auxiliary thresholds, including $[1/2,1]$. Scalar rescaling preserves
1521: $D_n^C(z)=D_n^Z(z)$, including zero energy. Its error is at most
1522: $Cga^3\Psi(x)$ by the Mills comparison. The sandwich and
1523: \eqref{g:threshold-payment}--\eqref{g:bad-center-energy} now give
1524: \begin{equation}\label{g:conditional-comparison}
1525:  |P(D_n(x)\mid\mathcal N_x)-d(x)|
1526:  =|P(D_n^A(x))-d(x)|\le C\Lambda\Psi(x)
1527:  \le CR_n(x)\Psi(x).
1528: \end{equation}
1529: All $Q_C=0$ samples lie in $E^c$. If $Q_A=0$, then
1530: $Q_C=d_0^2/n<v_0/2$, so this case is also paid. No zero-energy
1531: quotient occurs.
1532: 
1533: Since the given $x$ belongs to the unchanged host domain $I_n$,
1534: \eqref{g:host-discard}, with $H_0=\sigma\sqrt n h_x$, gives
1535: \[
1536:  B_{D,x}:=P(D_n(x),\mathcal N_x^c)
1537:  \le Cn\pi\Psi(x)\le C\epsilon_c^{-3}R_n(x)\Psi(x).
1538: \]
