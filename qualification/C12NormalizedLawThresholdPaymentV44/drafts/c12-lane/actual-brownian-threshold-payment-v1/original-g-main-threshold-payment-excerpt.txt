For $0<s<t$, the Brownian double-hit difference lies in
$\{s\le\max B<t\}\cup\{s\le-\min B<t\}$; reflection gives
$0\le d(s)-d(t)\le4\{\Psi(s)-\Psi(t)\}$.
Since $ah$ is small, the Mills bounds give $\Psi(x\pm h)\asymp\Psi(x)$ and
\begin{equation}\label{g:threshold-payment}
 |d(x\pm h)-d(x)|\le Cah\Psi(x)\le C\Lambda\Psi(x).
