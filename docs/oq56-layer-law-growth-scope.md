# Finite layer laws, the full observation model, and fixed p52 growth

The source constructions require actual three-state probabilities at every layer, an independent product observation containing G and all categorical coordinates, and one coefficient sequence fixed before any exponent or sample size is chosen. This milestone supplies those foundations and the growth identities needed for the later moment and rare-event arguments.

`ThreeStateLaw` constructs the actual atomic law with weights 1−p−q, p, q. Probability and weighted expectation formulas require the displayed nonnegative weights and total-mass condition. Every real observable and pair observable is genuinely integrable because these particular finite atomic measures are finite. The exact finite kernel has zero row integral when q≠0 and belongs to L2 under its actual pair law. No conditional or infinite-layer moment formula is postulated.

`LayeredProductLaw` constructs normalized Lebesgue volume on [−1,1], the countable product of the actual three-state marginals, and their independent product on ℝ × (ℕ → Fin 3). It proves the exact first, entire-sequence and coordinate marginals, interval support, independence of G from the entire categorical sequence, mutual independence of all categorical coordinates, distinct-coordinate pair laws and every finite-dimensional category law. Pointwise parameter inequalities prove the actual factor probabilities and exclude the infinite-product fallback branch. The categorical laws may differ across layers.

`FixedLayerCoefficients` fixes the p52 family using source J=j+6: N=2^(3J³), t=2^(2J³), c=1+1/J, τ=1/3−1/J, p=exp(−N^τ), q=N^(−c). All indices satisfy the proved positive probability bounds, and t=N^(2/3). The coefficients have no η, λ or sample-size argument. `FixedLayerGrowth` gives the increasing subsequence, C/τ limits, exact logarithm and dyadic identities, a positive eventual separation for every η∈(0,1/5), and the source scalar rare-cost limit N^(−1/J)(log N)²→0. A cutoff may depend on η without changing the family.
