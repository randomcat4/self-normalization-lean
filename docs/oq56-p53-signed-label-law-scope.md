# Scope of SparseSignedLabelLaw

The construction realizes one fixed category/fair-Bool product law from p53 §3. The product coordinate theorem proves actual IndepFun; equality of marginals alone is not used as an independence argument. The generic L1 equivalence first checks finite two-atom slices and then the outer norm series. The integral formula retains its Integrable premise.

This unit does not construct two independent tagged copies, the continuous noise A, G/Z, a real encoding, iid samples, the residual operator, positive true denominators, conditional events or final tail ratios. The signed real amplitude consumer remains a later source-bound unit. P53, OQ6 and RS2 stay OPEN.
