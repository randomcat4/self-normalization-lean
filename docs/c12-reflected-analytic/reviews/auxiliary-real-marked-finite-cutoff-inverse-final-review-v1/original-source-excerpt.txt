4431: We prove a master at any cutoff $b>0$. Put
4432: $a_0(s)=1$, $a_1(s)=a_3(s)$, $M_0=1$, $M_1=|X_1|^3$,
4433: and let $\gamma=-c$ if $E[A;A\le b]\ge1/2$, and $\gamma=C$
4434: otherwise. For $\ell\in\{0,1\}$, fixed $p\ge0,q>0$, and
4435: $H\in[b,\infty]$, the master is
4436: \begin{gather}
4437:  E[M_\ell|d|^pe^{-sT}]
4438:  \le C_ps^{-p/2}a_\ell(s/2)e^{\gamma s(t-\ell)},
4439:        \qquad0<s\le2/b,                              \label{cnx:dampedmoment}\\
4440:  E[1_{\{b\le T<H\}}M_\ell T^{-q/2}(1+|d|/\sqrt T)^p]
4441:  \le C_{p,q}\int_0^{1/b}s^{q/2-1}a_\ell(s/2)
4442:                    e^{\gamma(s+1/H)(t-\ell)}\,ds,      \label{cnx:dampedbudget}
4443: \end{gather}
4444: where $1/\infty=0$. No third moment is assumed.
4474: For $T\ge b$ and $\nu>0$,
4475: \[
4476:  T^{-\nu}\le C_\nu\int_0^{1/b}s^{\nu-1}e^{-sT}\,ds,
4477: \]
4478: by substituting $u=sT$, whose upper endpoint is at least one.
4479: Also $1_{\{T<H\}}\le e e^{-T/H}$. Apply the integral bound with
4480: $\nu=q/2$ and $(p+q)/2$, using
4481: $(1+v)^p\le C_p(1+v^p)$. In \eqref{cnx:dampedmoment} set
4482: $u=s+1/H\le2/b$; monotonicity gives
4483: $a_\ell(u/2)\le a_\ell(s/2)$ and $u^{-p/2}\le s^{-p/2}$.
4484: This proves \eqref{cnx:dampedbudget} by Tonelli; pointwise damped moments
4485: are finite under the second-moment assumption.
